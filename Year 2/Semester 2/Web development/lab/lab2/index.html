I love movies about the Internet. As someone who has grown up just as much on the web as off it, I feel as if it’s my virtual home, and inherently connect to films with social networks or hacking or viral media as major plot points perhaps more than films about any other subject.These movies thematically explore different aspects of the web, revealing both the positive and negative affects of living in such a connected world.

Please be aware that my list is strongly subjective
